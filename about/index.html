About

Quiet structure,
deliberate space.

North Window Light is an independent design studio. We design brand identities, packaging and editorial work for independent brands, cafés, shops and hotels. Working with clients worldwide.

A studio, not a résumé.

We work with grids, contrast and deliberate white space. Restraint is a tool for us, not a look. Everything we make has to work in print, on the shelf and in daily use, not only on screen.

We take on a complete identity or a single piece: a label, a menu, a book. We work with founders and creative leads who care more about precision than volume.

We send a first direction within 48 hours of receiving a complete brief. We work by email and text, so briefs and feedback stay clear. We take on a limited number of projects at a time.

If your brand speaks in structure, texture and quiet contrast, write to frigidbite@outlook.com.
