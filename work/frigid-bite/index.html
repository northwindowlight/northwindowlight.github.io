Jewellery · Digital flagship

FRIGID
BITE

Cultural relics, reframed.

We turned avant-garde jewellery into an online shop that feels like an archive. Brand visual, page design and Shopify build, end to end.

Date
2026
Type
Commission · Online store, live
Role
Brand visual · Web design · Shopify build
Scope
Homepage · Collection · Mobile experience

Problem

How do you sell jewellery without sounding like a sale?

The pieces are limited editions. We cut back promotional language and let facts carry the value: work numbers, materials and traces of making.

Approach

Jewellery as a digital archive

The key visual builds the world, the product grid is for browsing, and the craft chapter adds detail.

Scarcity through information, not decoration

The page moves from series atmosphere into product, material, making and brand notes, with a steady rhythm on mobile.

A craft data module states material age, time per piece and edition size.

Material studies

Mark, piece & paper

Outcome

From identity
to interface.

The brand visual runs through the Shopify homepage, product pages and content modules, and holds up on mobile. The store is now live.
