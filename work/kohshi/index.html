Fragrance · Self-initiated

KOHSHI

Scent of Silence

We turned "quiet" into a fragrance brand that can be produced and displayed. KOHSHI is our own brand study, covering identity, packaging specifications and retail extensions.

KOHSHI is a self-initiated brand study by North Window Light.

Project
Self-initiated, fictional brand
Role
Brand strategy · Visual design · Art direction
Scope
Identity · Packaging · Retail · Social
Output
20-page deck · Web edit

Problem

In a field of similar mood marketing, how can quiet be remembered?

A mood alone is easy to forget. We carried quiet into packaging sizes, labels and scenting materials, so the idea works in real use.

Approach

A system, not a mood

Ink black, warm white, moss green and grey-green set the colour. A wordmark, scent symbols and a label master hold the brand together.

Size, margin and material

The 70 × 110 mm label master includes bleed, trim and a safety area. It scales to shopping bags, perfume boxes and scent cards.

Quiet you can see

Forest, moss, dark water and natural side light make one image direction. Paper, stone, water and light set the material palette.

From shelf to screen

The wordmark breaks apart into editorial compositions for posters, retail displays and social pages.

Outcome

A brand
people can feel.

The full system is set out in a 20-page deck. The same rules run through packaging, scenting materials, retail and social.
