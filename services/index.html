Services

What we design.

We design a complete identity or a single, carefully made piece for independent brands and hospitality. Working with clients worldwide.

Posters, menus & spatial graphics

Posters, menus and environmental graphics for cafés, restaurants, hotels and cultural spaces. We set type and grid for viewing distance, lighting and print specs, and adapt one layout to social posts, notices and screens.

Selected work: Lumen Hill, INTERLUDE

Brand identity & packaging

Logo, typography, colour and grid, carried to labels, boxes, bags, cards and workwear. Every piece is specified for production, not only for the screen.

Selected work: KOHSHI, Kim Nhuan Wood, Luang Prabang Coffee

Fashion & editorial

Lookbooks, campaign images, book covers, interiors and publications. We use cropping, sequence and margin to keep a quiet tension across pages and screens.

Selected work: White Night, Poetry in Layers
