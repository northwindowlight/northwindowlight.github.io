Services & Engagement

We design a complete visual identity or a single, carefully made piece for independent brands, hospitality and cultural spaces. Every piece is specified for production, not only for the screen.

01 · Scope

Three Core Commissions

First direction within 48 hours · Production-ready files

I. Hospitality · Spatial · Cultural

Posters, Menus & Spatial Graphics

Posters, menus and environmental graphics for cafés, restaurants, hotels and cultural spaces. Type and grid are set for viewing distance, lighting and print specifications, and adapted cleanly across social posts, notices and screens.

Standard Deliverables:
  • • Posters & exhibition print files
  • • Seasonal printed menus & table cards
  • • Spatial graphics & signage specs
  • • Social & screen format adaptations
Selected work: Lumen Hill · INTERLUDE
II. Brand · Packaging · Retail

Brand Identity & Packaging

Logo, typography, colour and grid systems carried through to labels, boxes, bags, cards and workwear. Every piece is engineered for physical manufacturing and material quality.

Standard Deliverables:
  • • Vector logo & wordmark system
  • • Labels, boxes & packaging dielines
  • • Colour, type & material production specs
  • • Cards, bags & workwear extensions
Selected work: KOHSHI · Kim Nhuan Wood · Luang Prabang Coffee
III. Publishing · Fashion · Editorial

Fashion & Editorial

Lookbooks, campaign imagery, book covers, interior layouts and publications. Deliberate cropping, sequence and generous margins maintain a quiet tension across print spreads and screens.

Standard Deliverables:
  • • Lookbooks & brand catalogues
  • • Book covers, interior grids & typography
  • • Print & screen reading sequence
  • • Print-ready artwork & web asset packs
Selected work: White Night · Poetry in Layers
